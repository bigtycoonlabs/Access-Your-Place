# Planned announcements — not yet written, not yet sent

Recorded 9 August 2026 so this is not lost between sessions. **Both go out once building is
done, not before.** Nothing here has been sent.

## 1. The client platform update

Goes to **every client file** — all 475, not only the 14 with accounts. Most of the book has
never seen the platform, and that is exactly who this is for.

**The thing that has to be answered, directly:** there is talk that the company is dying or
under heavy legal pressure. It is not true. The message should not sound defensive or
mention the rumour as a rumour — it should simply be so specific about what has been built
and what is next that the rumour reads as obviously wrong to anyone who gets it.

What it needs to cover:

- **Where we have been.** Five years, and the number of closings, master leases, landlords
  negotiated with and vendors — figures the owner supplies, not figures derived from the
  database. The database is not the history of the business.
- **What has been built.** Free market research from Penny, the deal marketplace where every
  listing has had a human speak to the landlord, Property Forge for sourcing your own,
  credits, the knowledge library.
- **Free versus paid, stated plainly.** Research and the library are free whether they ever
  buy or not. What costs money is the deal.
- **What has changed** — including anything on terms of service.
- **What is staying the same.**
- **The honest framing:** stronger, tighter workflows, more technical capability, harder
  processes, here for the long haul.

Tone: not a newsletter and not a defence. A letter from an operator who has been heads-down
and is telling their clients what got built.

## 2. The Success Team update

Separate message, different content. Covers the new workflows and what each role can now do:

- The **client book** — 475 relationships, ownership, logging a touch, next steps
- **Alerts and routing** — admin owns disputes, legal and compliance; acquisition owns deals
  and arrivals; setup owns launches; owners see everything
- **Deal presentation** — everything except the address, released with credits or cash
- **Both an acquisition and a setup manager** can hold the same client
- **Payout details and agreements** — where commission goes, what needs signing
- Changes to role responsibilities

## Why not now

The owner's instruction: finish building first. Announcing a platform while it is still
moving means the message is wrong by the time it lands — and this book has heard enough
that is not quite right.
