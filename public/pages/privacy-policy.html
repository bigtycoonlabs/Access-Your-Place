Privacy Policy

How Access Your Place collects, protects, redacts, and discloses information across the platform.

1. Information We Collect

To operate the Access Your Place platform — including account creation, deal matching, Acquisition Manager pairing, and the investor or landlord portal — we collect information you provide directly, such as your name, business entity details, contact information, financial qualification details relevant to acquisition funding, and communications with your Acquisition Manager, Setup Manager, or the Success Team.

We also collect operational data generated through your use of the platform: property inquiries, deal-flow activity, portal credit balances, document signatures, and platform usage logs.

2. Property and Operator History Data

As part of our Standard of Truth, YP transfers 100% of known material facts about a property's operational history to an incoming Client — including raw Profit and Loss statements, operational ledgers, and reservation calendar screenshots from past or current operators, where available.

Redaction Commitment: Before any such data is transferred, YP completely blocks off, redacts, or removes personal names, company names, distinct branding, financial account numbers, or any identifying entity data associated with the previous operator. The data transferred belongs to the asset's history; the identity of the prior operator remains confidential.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to match you with a dedicated Acquisition Manager and, where applicable, a Setup Manager; to process account funding, platform credits, and referral incentives; to communicate deal flow, market data, and Penny AI-generated projections relevant to your portfolio; to maintain accurate transaction, lease, and sublease records; and to satisfy legal, accounting, and compliance obligations.

4. Confidentiality Within the Network

The performance, underperformance, or structural setup of any operator's business is treated as strictly confidential within the network, even among operators in the same community. This information is never shared with other Clients, Landlords, or operators except as required to fulfill the Standard of Truth disclosure described above for a specific, presented deal.

5. Disclosure to Service Providers

We share information with the vendors, payment processors, and service providers necessary to operate your transaction — including background screening and application processors used for landlord and community qualification, payment processors for wire, Zelle, Venmo, or Cash App transactions, and independent Vendor Network partners you choose to engage. These vendors are independent operators, not YP employees, and are not bound by YP's internal data handling protocols.

6. Law Enforcement & Legal Disclosure

While YP maintains an ironclad commitment to privacy, data redaction, and user anonymity under normal commercial operations, all privacy protections are immediately waived in the event of criminal investigations or illegal activity.

YP maintains a strict, zero-tolerance policy for fraud, identity theft, money laundering, human trafficking, or any other criminal enterprise that creates corporate liability. In the event that a law enforcement agency, regulatory body, or court of competent jurisdiction issues a formal subpoena, warrant, or official request for information regarding a Client or transaction, Set Up Your Place LLC will cooperate fully — including turning over transaction histories, payment ledgers, digital timestamps, communication histories, Master Leases, Corporate Subleases, and Acquisition Agreements.

7. Data Retention

We retain account, transaction, and compliance records for as long as your platform account remains active, and for the period required afterward to satisfy accounting, tax, legal, and dispute-resolution obligations.

8. Your Choices

You may request a copy of the personal information associated with your account, request correction of inaccurate information, or request account closure, by contacting the Success Team at success@accessyourplace.com. Certain records — including those related to completed transactions, leases, and subleases — may be retained even after account closure as required by law or contract.

9. International Clients

Overseas and international investors using the platform acknowledge that data may be processed and stored in the United States, and that all market data, analytics, and consultative insights are provided strictly on an "as-is" and "where-is" basis consistent with our Terms of Service.

10. Changes to This Policy

Access Your Place reserves the right to update, modify, or alter this Privacy Policy at any time without prior individual notice. The latest version will be maintained on the YP platform. Continued use of the platform constitutes agreement to the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the Success Team at success@accessyourplace.com or by calling (813) 303-9110.
