We're hiring Acquisition Managers and Setup Managers.

Join the team that's actually building the engine — not selling a course about it. Get certified, get trained across both disciplines, and build your own track record placing corporate leases and launching operations across our network.

Open Roles

Two roles. One certification path that connects them.

Every Acquisition Manager and Setup Manager goes through the same rigorous training program. Many of our team members end up certified in both — the skill sets overlap more than people expect.

Open
Acquisition Manager

Source deals. Negotiate leases. Build your book.

You'll analyze the marketplace, run the 80/20 micro-market valuation on every deal, negotiate directly with landlords, and place corporate leases for clients across our network — on-market, off-market, and through our Master Lease program.

Build and own your client pipeline
Direct landlord and property manager negotiations
Use Penny's real market data, not guesswork
Run your own furnished rental portfolio alongside the role
Open
Setup Manager

Launch operations. Coordinate logistics. Get units live.

You'll manage furniture and decor procurement, coordinate with housekeepers and maintenance providers on the ground, handle Wi-Fi and utility setup, and connect clients to the vendors and software they need — for furnished and unfurnished properties alike.

Own the full launch process, start to finish
Build relationships with vendors across your market
Work refresher packages for existing operations too
Direct line into the certification track for AM as well

Cross-train. Get certified in both.

Acquisition Managers can train and certify as Setup Managers, and Setup Managers can train and certify as Acquisition Managers. Most of our highest-performing team members hold both certifications — it means you can carry a client from sourcing straight through launch without a handoff.

Earning Potential

You're building your own book — your earnings scale with it.

Acquisition Managers and Setup Managers earn on a commission structure tied to deals sourced, deals closed, and operations launched — not a flat hourly rate.

Acquisition Manager Commission

Structure
Commission
Sourcing a deal
15% of the acquisition fee
Closing a deal
15% of the acquisition fee
Sourced and closed the same deal
30% total
Found a deal listed on the marketplace, regardless of who closes it
15% sourcing commission, paid either way

Acquisition Managers can source both furnished and unfurnished opportunities and list them on the marketplace. Your 15% sourcing commission is yours once you've found the deal — whether you personally close it or another team member does.

Setup Manager Compensation

Project Type
Compensation
Furnished deal requiring a refresher package
15% commission
Unfurnished property launch (client-requested setup)
$750 – $1,800 flat fee per launch
Other assigned projects
Flat setup management fee, set per project

Setup Managers are typically assigned to most furnished deals and to unfurnished launches where a client has requested setup support. Flat fees are set per project; furnished refresher packages are commission-based.
