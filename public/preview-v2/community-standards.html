Community Standards

How every Client, Landlord, vendor, and staff member is expected to conduct themselves inside the Access Your Place network.

Applies to: All Clients, Landlords, Vendors, Referral Partners, and Staff
Enforced by: The YP Success Team
Governing Document: Incorporated by reference into the full Terms of Service, Section 15

1. Collaboration Is Key

The foundational doctrine of the Access Your Place network is that collaboration is key. We operate a supportive, high-level professional network designed to foster mutual growth and shared wealth generation — not a zero-sum competition between operators.

Overflow Lead Sharing: Operators who capture overflow guest inquiries, localized corporate contract leads, or unmapped market opportunities are urged to submit them directly to the YP Success Team. The Success Team dynamically distributes these overflow opportunities to other active network clients operating within that specific area or community, driving collective ecosystem growth.

2. Anti-Gossip and Anti-Slander Mandates

To preserve a professional environment, YP maintains a strict, zero-tolerance policy against malicious behavior, gossip, and network disruption.

Gossip Prohibitions: The relative success, underperformance, operational mechanics, or structural status of an independent operator's unit — even when operating within the exact same multifamily community or under the same landlord — is completely confidential. Gossip, rumor-spreading, misleading reporting, backchannel speculation, or monitoring the operational status of another user's business is strictly prohibited.

Slander Enforcement: Any engagement in unnecessary slander, public defamation, or corporate gossip within the network will result in an immediate, permanent ban from the Access Your Place platform. We encourage all platform and network users to submit any grievances, disputes, issues, and complaints to the Success Team for resolution rather than airing them within the network.

Landlord Conduct Standards: Landlords and property managers who interface with the network are held to identical professional standards. Any landlord or community staff member operating under a Master Lease framework who spreads rumors, delivers false accusations, or discloses unit-specific financial or operational information from unit to unit will be subject to immediate removal, lease evaluation, and a permanent platform restriction.

3. Pre-Connection Landlord Outreach Ban

Pre-Connection Outreach Ban: Reaching out to, calling, emailing, or visiting landlords or leasing offices before an Acquisition Manager has formally matched the Client and authorized the connection is a direct violation of these standards. Unauthorized contact can disrupt ongoing corporate negotiations and jeopardize active deals for other network operators at that same community.

Post-Payment Outreach Authorization: The Client is permitted to communicate directly with the landlord or community staff only after they have processed their account activation fee, executed the property-specific Acquisition Agreement, and submitted their formal corporate leasing application.

4. We Fix Crowns, We Don't Spread Rumors

This is more than a courtesy — it's a core value. The relative success, underperformance, or structural setup of another operator's business, even if they're running in the exact same apartment community, is strictly confidential. Malicious gossip, backchannel rumors, and public defamation are bad for business and destroy ecosystems.

Unnecessary slander or rumor-spreading is grounds for immediate removal and a permanent ban from the Access Your Place network. We do not engage with corporate bullying or toxicity. The Success Team is entirely dedicated to handling and mediating companywide disputes between Clients, Landlords, and staff through calm, structured, professional conversation.

5. The Legal Action Dispute Shield

If a Client threatens to initiate litigation, files an unverified formal legal dispute, or submits a hostile legal claim against Access Your Place, Set Up Your Place LLC, or its internal staff members, YP will immediately deploy its Legal Action Shield.

Immediate Service Cessation: YP will immediately freeze and halt all active services, consultations, setup processing, administrative resources, and platform access for that Client.

Communication Freeze: All direct communication channels with YP specialists will be severed, and all interactions will be routed exclusively through corporate legal counsel while the dispute is in progress.

6. Law Enforcement Cooperation

While YP maintains an ironclad commitment to privacy, data redaction, and user anonymity under normal commercial operations, all privacy protections are immediately waived in the event of criminal investigations or illegal activity.

YP maintains a strict, zero-tolerance policy for fraud, identity theft, money laundering, human trafficking, or any other criminal enterprise that creates corporate liability. In the event that a law enforcement agency, regulatory body, or court of competent jurisdiction issues a formal subpoena, warrant, or official request for information regarding a Client or transaction, Set Up Your Place LLC will cooperate fully.

7. Vendor Network Conduct

All entities listed within the Vendor Network Directory are entirely independent operators. They do not work for Access Your Place, are not employees of the Company, and are not bound by YP's internal rules, operational protocols, or employee standards. It is the sole responsibility of the Client to vet, interview, contractually bind, and audit any vendors they choose to engage through the network.

Any vendor application requesting the listing of intellectual property products, educational courses, consulting coaches, trading gurus, or knowledge-based mentorship programs is subject to extended Success Team evaluation, and may be unconditionally rejected to protect network users from predatory or low-quality educational products.

8. Reporting a Concern

Any grievance, dispute, or complaint — whether client-to-client, landlord-to-client, client-to-landlord, or staff-related — should be routed directly to the Success Team rather than discussed publicly within the network. The Success Team exists specifically to mediate these situations through calm, structured, professional conversation, and is the centralized administrative backbone for compliance and dispute resolution companywide.
